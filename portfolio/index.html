Frequently Asked Questions

How soon should we book you for our wedding?

We recommend booking 12-18 months in advance, especially for popular summer dates. However, we sometimes have availability for last-minute bookings.

What's included in your wedding packages?

All packages include high-resolution digital images, a 10-page storybook album, and our signature unobtrusive coverage. We offer 3, 6, 8, and 10-hour options.

Do you travel across all of Cornwall?

Yes! We cover weddings from Bude to Penzance and everywhere in between. Travel within Cornwall is included in all packages.

What happens if it rains on our wedding day?

We're experts at creating beautiful photos in any weather. We always have backup plans and can work magic with Cornwall's famous moody skies.
