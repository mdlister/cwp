1 What Are Cookies?

Simple Explanation

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device to remember your preferences, login details, and other information that makes your browsing experience smoother.

Like a digital memory for websites

On Our Wedding Site

We use cookies to understand how couples like you use our site, which helps us improve our Cornwall wedding photography services and create better content for future brides and grooms.

2 Types of Cookies We Use

Always Active

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for our website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas.

Examples:

  • Session management (keeping you logged in during your visit)
  • Security features and fraud prevention
  • Load balancing across servers
Requires Consent

Analytics & Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website, allowing us to improve our services and content for Cornwall wedding couples.

What we learn:

  • Most popular Cornwall wedding venues visitors view
  • How long couples spend researching photography styles
  • Which blog posts are most helpful for wedding planning
These cookies are set by Google Analytics and help us create better content for you.
Preference-based

Functionality Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make (like your region or font size) to provide a more personalized experience on our wedding photography site.

Remembers:

  • Your cookie consent preferences
  • Whether you've dismissed our welcome message
  • Your preferred language or region settings

5 Questions & Contact

Common Questions

Will disabling cookies break the website? ▼

Essential cookies are required for basic functions. Non-essential cookies can be disabled, but some features (like analytics) won't work.

How often should I review my preferences? ▼

We recommend reviewing your preferences every 6 months. Our consent banner will reappear after this period.

Do you use cookies for advertising? ▼

No. As a Cornwall wedding photography business, we don't use advertising cookies. We focus on providing helpful content, not ads.
