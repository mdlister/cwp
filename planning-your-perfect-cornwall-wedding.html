Discover essential tips for planning a memorable Cornwall wedding, from choosing coastal venues to navigating unpredictable weather and creating stunning photographs that last a lifetime.

After photographing over 120 weddings across Cornwall’s stunning coastline, I’ve gathered the insights every couple needs for their perfect day.

There’s something truly magical about a Cornish wedding. The dramatic cliffs, golden beaches, and historic venues create a backdrop unlike anywhere else in the UK. But planning a wedding here comes with unique considerations that can make or break your day.

Why Cornwall Weddings Are Special

Cornwall isn’t just a location; it’s a feeling. The moment you step onto a Cornish cliff top as the sun sets over the Atlantic, you understand why couples travel from across the country to say “I do” here.

“Cornwall doesn’t just host your wedding - it becomes part of your love story. The sea, the light, the landscape all weave themselves into your memories.”

The Coastal Advantage

  • Natural Beauty: Every venue comes with a built-in backdrop
  • Unique Venues: From castle ruins to converted engine houses
  • Light Quality: Cornwall’s light is legendary among photographers

Choosing Your Cornwall Wedding Venue

This is arguably the most important decision you’ll make. The venue sets the tone for everything from guest experience to photography.

Coastal vs. Countryside

Pro Tip: Consider your guest list when choosing between coastal and countryside venues. Coastal venues often involve more travel but offer incredible photo opportunities.

Coastal Venues like St Michael’s Mount or Trebah Garden offer:

  • Dramatic sea views
  • Easy access for beach photos
  • Potential weather challenges
  • Often more expensive

Countryside Venues such as Boconnoc Estate provide:

  • Sheltered locations
  • Traditional Cornish charm
  • Easier logistics
  • Beautiful gardens

Top 5 Cornwall Venues We Love

  1. Trebah Garden - Tropical paradise with private beach
  2. Bodmin Jail - Unique, atmospheric conversion
  3. The Cornwall Hotel - Luxury with spa facilities
  4. St Enodoc Church - Iconic coastal church
  5. Pentillie Castle - Romantic riverside setting

Navigating Cornwall’s Weather

Let’s be honest - Cornwall’s weather can be unpredictable. But with the right planning, even rain can add to your wedding story.

Seasonal Considerations

Spring (March-May)

  • Pros: Wildflowers, fewer tourists, reasonable prices
  • Cons: Unpredictable weather, cooler temperatures
  • Photography tip: Spring light is soft and romantic - perfect for portraits

Summer (June-August)

  • Pros: Warmest weather, long evenings
  • Cons: Peak tourist season, higher prices
  • Photography tip: Golden hour lasts longer - schedule couple photos accordingly

Autumn (September-November)

  • Pros: Dramatic skies, autumn colors, fewer crowds
  • Cons: Shorter days, potential storms
  • Photography tip: Moody autumn light creates incredible atmosphere

Winter (December-February)

  • Pros: Cosy venues, dramatic coastal scenery
  • Cons: Limited daylight, potential venue closures
  • Photography tip: Embrace the mood - winter weddings have incredible atmosphere
