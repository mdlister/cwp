Why Choose Cornwall for Your Wedding?

Cornwall offers some of the UK's most dramatic and romantic wedding backdrops. From the rugged cliffs of Land's End to the subtropical gardens of the south coast, each location tells its own story. As Cornwall wedding specialists, we understand how to work with:

  • Coastal light - Cornwall's unique quality of light creates magical golden hours
  • Weather patterns - We know backup locations for every forecast
  • Venue logistics - From remote cliff-top ceremonies to historic manor houses
  • Seasonal considerations - Each month offers different advantages

With over 11 years photographing Cornish weddings, we've developed techniques to capture authentic moments regardless of conditions, ensuring your wedding photos reflect the genuine emotion of your day.

Our Photography Philosophy

Documentary Storytelling

We believe the best wedding photos come from genuine moments, not posed scenarios. Our approach is unobtrusive - we blend into your day to capture the real laughter, tears, and joy as they naturally unfold.
