🌿 Cornwall Wedding Photography — Brand Foundation

Brand Identity

Core Values

Artisanal Storytelling: (Evolves from “Storytelling”) Framing the photography as a crafted art, not just a service.

Unwavering Excellence: (New) Communicates a commitment to the highest quality in every detail, from shooting to delivery.

Heartfelt Connection: (Evolves from “Kindness”) Feels more profound and relationship-focused.

Effortless Experience: (Evolves from “Simplicity”) Positions the simplicity as a luxurious benefit for the couple.

Brand Personality

If Cornwall Wedding Photography were a person, it would be: - Calm and confident, like a trusted friend who brings reassurance to a busy day.\

  • Friendly and insightful, offering gentle direction when needed.\
  • Lightly witty, with a touch of British humour that keeps things relaxed.\
  • Professional yet approachable, never pushy or over the top.

Three words that define the brand:
> Cool as a cucumber. Simple. Honourable.

Tone of Voice

  • Slightly witty, always respectful.\
  • Warm and human, avoiding jargon or clichés.\
  • Professional yet conversational, creating comfort and trust.\
  • Avoids: overly “salesy” or cheesy wedding talk, heavy sarcasm, or anything that sounds cheap.

Visual Style

  • Colour Palette: Coastal tones inspired by Cornwall — soft pastels, sunset yellows and oranges, sandy neutrals, and sea-washed blues.\
  • Photography Style: Bright, colourful, and candid — no gimmicks, no trends that date quickly.\
  • Design Language: Clean layouts, balanced whitespace, and consistent typography for an uncluttered, easy-to-navigate feel.

Color Palette: Coastal Sophistication

This palette moves beyond soft pastels to deeper, more elegant tones that feel premium and timeless.

Primary Colors:

Deep Ocean Navy: #0A1A2F (For headers, primary buttons, key accents - conveys trust and depth)

Warm Stone: #E8DFD8 (For backgrounds - a warm, elegant neutral)

Secondary/Accent Colors:

Sandy Beige: #C9B7A1 (Secondary backgrounds, borders)

Dusty Sage: #8A9B8A (Accents, subtle highlights - earthy and calm)

Sunset Blush: #D4A5A5 (Very subtle accents on interactive elements - a touch of warmth)

Neutrals:

Pure White: #FFFFFF

Charcoal Grey: #2F2F2F (For body text - more premium than pure black)

Typography: Elegant and Readable

Headings (Serif for Elegance): Playfair Display

Why: It has a beautiful, classic, and high-end feel that is perfect for a luxury brand.

Body Text (Sans-Serif for Clarity): Inter

Why: A clean, highly readable, modern sans-serif that pairs perfectly with Playfair Display and works excellently on the web.

Brand Experience

Across every touchpoint — website, social media, print — the experience should feel:

  • Calm and easy to navigate
  • Warm and engaging in tone
  • Consistent in look and feel
  • Focused on trust, care, and connection

Brand Positioning Statement

For couples getting married in Cornwall who value authenticity, simplicity, and heartfelt storytelling, Cornwall Wedding Photography offers unobtrusive, candid coverage that captures every meaningful moment without fuss. With transparent pricing, calm professionalism, and a timeless coastal aesthetic, we help you stay present on your wedding day — while we quietly capture the memories you’ll cherish for a lifetime.

Vision & Mission Statements

Vision

To become Cornwall’s most trusted and loved wedding photography brand — known for capturing bright, candid, and meaningful moments along the Cornish coast, while providing an effortless and genuinely caring experience for every couple.

Mission

To tell each couple’s story through my lens as a fly on the wall — creating simple, bright, and timeless images that become lasting heirlooms.

Key Messages & Taglines

Primary Tagline:
> Be present — I’ll do the rest.

Supporting Messages:\

  • Simple. Bright. Candid.\
  • Moments that become heirlooms.\
  • Premium experience, approachable pricing.\
  • Calm, friendly, and quietly everywhere you need me.\
  • Unobtrusive storytelling for Cornwall weddings.

Business Plan Outline (Draft)

1. Executive Summary

Cornwall Wedding Photography is a new, locally focused photography brand capturing authentic, candid wedding moments across Cornwall. Building on over 11 years of experience through Mike Lister Photography, the brand aims to establish a distinct identity with a calm, transparent, and heartfelt approach.

Business Goals (Next 1–3 Years): - 10 bookings in 2026, scaling to 20 per year within three years.\

  • Rank in the top 5 Google search results for “Cornwall wedding photography.”\
  • Maintain 5★ reviews on Google.\
  • Build a strong Cornwall-focused website and SEO presence.

2. Products & Services

  • Time-Based Packages: 3, 6, 8, or 10-hour coverage options.\
  • Inclusions: USB delivery, 10-page printed storybook album.\
  • Optional Add-ons: Second shooter, drone photography, engagement session.\
  • Turnaround: Full gallery within three weeks; sneak peeks on the wedding day.

3. Market Analysis

  • Market Overview: Cornwall’s wedding market is highly competitive, ranging from part-time photographers to high-end studios.\
  • Target Audience: Relaxed, outdoorsy professionals (teachers, nurses, creatives) who value trust, transparency, and authentic storytelling over trends.\
  • Competitor Landscape: Many focus on social media presence and styled shoots; few emphasise transparent pricing and personal connection.

Opportunity: A locally grounded, approachable, premium-but-fair brand focused solely on Cornwall, SEO-optimised and story-driven.

4. Marketing & Brand Strategy

  • Website: Clean, coastal design optimised for SEO.\
  • Social Media: Showcase real weddings and candid stories, avoiding heavy filters or trends.\
  • Client Experience: Personal calls, venue walk-throughs, Christmas cards, and ongoing relationship building.\
  • Trust Building: 5★ Google reviews, transparent communication, fast turnaround.

5. Operations & Delivery

  • Owner/Photographer: Mike Lister\
  • Coverage Area: Entire Cornish coast and countryside\
  • Workflow: Pre-wedding consultation → wedding day coverage → same-day sneak peeks → full gallery delivery in 3 weeks\
  • Customer Relationship: Emphasis on care, communication, and after-service follow-up.

6. Financial Overview

  • Pricing Structure: Transparent, time-based.\
  • Revenue Streams: Wedding photography packages, albums, prints, optional add-ons.\
  • Growth Strategy: Gradual increase in bookings and pricing as brand awareness grows.

7. Long-Term Vision

By year five, Cornwall Wedding Photography will be recognised as a leading name in Cornish wedding storytelling — synonymous with authenticity, kindness, and timeless imagery. The brand will maintain a healthy balance of creativity, family life, and sustainable business growth.

We fix that. We’re Cornwall’s unobtrusive storytellers who capture how love felt on your perfect day. No awkward posing, no missing the magic.
