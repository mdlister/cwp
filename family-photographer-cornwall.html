Family Photographer Cornwall

Natural, relaxed family photography on Cornwall's most beautiful beaches

Family photography should feel natural, not forced. No stiff poses, no awkward smiles—just genuine moments of connection captured on Cornwall's stunning coastline.

I'm a Cornwall-based family photographer specialising in relaxed, documentary-style sessions on the beach. Whether you're splashing in the waves, building sandcastles, or exploring the dunes with your children, I capture your family exactly as you are.

Why choose a beach family session?

  • Natural, candid moments – Children are happiest on the beach. Running, playing, exploring—I capture the real joy of your family.
  • Stunning Cornwall backdrops – Golden sand, dramatic cliffs, and ever-changing light create beautiful, timeless images.
  • No awkward posing – I work unobtrusively, letting your family be themselves while I document the moments that matter.
  • A relaxed experience – Beach sessions feel like a day out, not a photoshoot. No stress, just quality time together.

Real family sessions: Charlie, Rhys & their children

I first met Charlie and Rhys when I photographed their wedding at Tredudwell Manor in Fowey. It was a beautiful summer celebration, full of joy and laughter. Fast forward a few years, and they now have two gorgeous children—and I had the privilege of photographing their family on the beach at Perranporth.

Perranporth is one of Cornwall's most spectacular beaches. With its wide stretch of golden sand, dramatic dunes, and the iconic Chapel Rock, it offers endless opportunities for beautiful family photos.

Charlie and Rhys wanted images that captured their family as they are—playful, loving, and full of energy. We spent the session on the beach and in the dunes, letting their children run, explore, and be themselves. The result was a collection of images that feel authentic and timeless.

Where can we shoot?

I photograph family sessions across Cornwall's most beautiful locations. Some of my favourites include:

  • Perranporth – Wide golden sands, dramatic dunes, and Chapel Rock—perfect for playful family sessions.
  • Holywell Bay – Beautiful dunes, sweeping sands, and stunning Atlantic views.
  • St Ives Harbour – Charming streets, galleries, and beach views with a relaxed coastal feel.
  • Gwithian Towans – Miles of golden sand and dunes, perfect for children to explore.
  • Trevenna Barns – Rustic countryside charm near St Neot, ideal for families who prefer inland settings.

I'm always open to new locations. If you have a beach or spot that's special to your family, I'd love to capture you there.
