OUR APPROACH

Why Trevenna Barns is so special

Within the Rowe family for hundreds of years and lovingly opened for over 20 years, Trevenna has grown from a family farm into a place where unforgettable weddings come to life. At its heart is the Farm Kitchen, where homegrown and locally sourced produce is crafted into seasonal menus—often slow-cooked in a wood-fired oven—offering guests a true taste of Cornwall. The recently added Mowhay provides a stunning new space for wedding breakfasts, ceremonies, or creative gatherings. Whether it's an intimate celebration beneath the barn beams or portraits amongst the established gardens and rolling countryside, every moment at Trevenna feels personal and grounded in nature.

What to expect:

  • Natural, candid moments captured unobtrusively
  • Focus on authentic emotions and connections
  • Working with Trevenna Barns's unique light and landscape
  • Timeless images that tell your unique story
